LOCKDMAX

PRIVACY POLICY · EFFECTIVE 10 NOVEMBER 2026

Effective 10 November 2026. LOCKDMAX is made by one person, Mário Otruba, a private individual in Slovakia. Your data lives on your phone. Two things can leave it: a backup to your account, only if you sign in and agree, and the meals and tasks sent for an AI estimate, which you can turn off. This policy says what, why, where and for how long. LOCKDMAX is for adults 18 and over.

What the app stores on your phone

Your lock, habits, things you're cutting out and the daily ticks, plan tasks, training splits and sessions, the food and weight you log, the sex, age, height, activity and goal you enter for a calorie budget, journal and gratitude entries, daily scores, and your settings. It lives in the app's private storage on this device. A plain JSON copy sits in the app's folder, which you can open in the Files app (On My iPhone › LOCKDMAX); before a reset the app also keeps up to three dated safety copies there. Delete the app and all of it goes with it.

Optional account and backup

Signing in (with Apple or Google) is optional, and you have to tick that you agree first. It creates a LOCKDMAX account holding an account ID and, if the provider shares them, your name and email (with Apple's Hide My Email, a relay address). While you're signed in, the app uploads a copy of everything listed above, together with your device model and app version, so you can restore it on another phone. Some of it is health information, and your Clean list and reading choice can say something about your sex life or beliefs; that's why we ask for your explicit consent. The backup is stored by our database provider, Supabase, in the EU (Frankfurt), encrypted in transit and at rest, and used for nothing but giving it back to you. Supabase's sign-in system also records your IP address, device type and sign-in times, to keep accounts secure. Sign out any time to stop backups. Settings › Account › Delete account deletes the account and its backup for good (for Apple accounts it also disconnects LOCKDMAX from your Apple ID); what's on your phone stays.

AI estimates

AI estimates are on from the start, and you can turn them off any time in Settings › AI. With them on: a meal you describe or photograph (with any note you add) is sent to estimate calories and macros, and a task you add to your plan is sent with your lock goal and habit names to rate how hard it is. It goes through our server to Anthropic (the company that makes Claude) in the United States. Your name and email are never sent, and location data is removed from photos. Our server checks your App Store subscription first; that proof of purchase carries its transaction ID and can carry your LOCKDMAX account ID. We don't store the text or photo. To enforce the daily limit, our server keeps a count per subscription per day, deleted after two days. Anthropic processes the request under its commercial terms and data processing agreement, doesn't use it to train its models, and keeps it only for a limited time for safety and abuse monitoring. Estimates are approximate; you can change any number. With AI off, or if it can't be reached, Apple's on-device model or your own number is used instead.

Notifications

Reminders are local notifications scheduled on your phone. No push server is involved. The evening Clean reminder counts what's left to tick and never names it.

Subscriptions

LOCKDMAX Pro is sold through Apple's In-App Purchase. Apple handles payment, renewal, cancellation and refunds; we never see your payment details. Subscriptions are managed with RevenueCat (RevenueCat, Inc., USA): it keeps a record of your purchases and whether LOCKDMAX Pro is active, under a random ID or, once you sign in, your LOCKDMAX account ID, so the subscription can be checked and restored on a new phone. No account is needed to buy.

Camera and photos

The camera is used only when you photograph a meal, and the photo library only when you choose a meal photo from it. That one photo is sent for the estimate and not kept by us. If you tap Download on a day card, the app asks to add that one image to your photo library.

What we don't do

No analytics, no crash-reporting SDKs, no advertising, no tracking. Your data is never sold, shared for advertising, or used for anything but the features above. The only counting: per day, how many people reach each of the first screens (for example how many saw the plans or started a subscription), kept as plain totals with nothing about who you are or your phone.

Legal bases (EU and UK)

Running the features you ask for and your subscription: contract (GDPR Art. 6(1)(b)). The backup, which can include health and other sensitive information: your explicit consent (Art. 6(1)(a) and 9(2)(a)), which you can withdraw any time by signing out or deleting the account; withdrawing doesn't affect what was done before. AI estimates: providing the feature you use (Art. 6(1)(b)); a meal, photo or task goes out only when you enter one, and turning AI off in Settings stops it. Sign-in security records and abuse limits: legitimate interest in keeping the service safe (Art. 6(1)(f)).

Who receives data, and where

Supabase (database and sign-in, EU), Anthropic (AI estimates, United States), RevenueCat (subscription records, United States), the last two under the EU Standard Contractual Clauses in their data processing agreements; Apple (Sign in with Apple, purchases) and Google (Google sign-in) as independent services under their own terms. Nobody else.

How long

On your phone: until you delete it or the app. Backup and account: until you delete the account. AI requests: not stored by us; the daily count, two days. Subscription records at RevenueCat: while you're a customer and as its terms require after. Sign-in security records: kept by Supabase for a limited period. Emails to support: until the matter is closed.

Your rights

Everything LOCKDMAX holds about you is visible in the app, and the copy in Files is a full export. You can correct anything in the app, withdraw consent as above, and delete your account and its backup any time. You can also ask us what we hold, for a copy, for correction or erasure, or object, and we'll answer within 30 days. EU and UK: you can complain to your data protection authority; ours is the Slovak Office for Personal Data Protection (dataprotection.gov.sk). Canada: you can withdraw consent and complain to the Office of the Privacy Commissioner of Canada or, in Québec, the Commission d'accès à l'information; the person responsible for privacy at LOCKDMAX is Mário Otruba.

United States

We don't sell or share personal information, or use it for targeted advertising. California residents can ask to know, correct or delete what we hold, and we won't treat you differently for asking. Consumer health data (Washington, Nevada and similar laws): the health-related data described above (weight, body details, food, training and your Clean list) is collected only to provide the features you use, is shared only with Supabase and, for AI estimates, Anthropic as described (RevenueCat only ever sees purchases), and is never sold. You can see and delete it in the app, withdraw consent, or write to us to exercise your rights.

Children

LOCKDMAX is for adults 18 and over and is not directed at children. If we learn that an account belongs to someone under 18, we delete it.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be in the app and at this address with a new effective date. If a change needs your consent, the app will ask again.

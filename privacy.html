LOCKD

PRIVACY POLICY · EFFECTIVE 16 SEPTEMBER 2026

LOCKD is a personal accountability app. The short version: your data stays on your phone. There is no LOCKD account, no LOCKD server, and nothing in the app sends your habits, journal, plan or scores anywhere. This page spells out the details.

What the app stores

Everything you enter or the app calculates is stored on your device only, in the app's private storage:

  • Your lock (start date and length), habits, things you're cutting out, and the daily ticks.
  • Plan tasks, rep counts, journal and gratitude entries, and daily scores.
  • Settings, including which notifications you want.

The app writes a plain-text backup of this data into its own Documents folder on your phone so you can copy it off with the Files app. It stays on the device unless you move it.

If you delete the app, all of this is deleted with it.

Apple Health

If you connect Health, LOCKD reads your steps, active energy, exercise minutes, workouts, resting heart rate, weight, and the calories, protein, carbohydrate and fat you've logged, for the current day and the days of your lock, to score your day and to work out your calorie budget. Food and weight you log in LOCKD are written to Health as dietary energy, protein, carbohydrate, fat and body mass so they live in one place. Health data is read on your device, stored only in the app's private storage, and never transmitted. It is never used for advertising or shared with anyone. You can revoke access at any time in iPhone Settings › Health › Data Access & Devices.

Calorie estimates

When you describe food in words, the estimate is produced by Apple's on-device language model on your iPhone. The text does not leave the device. Estimates are approximate; you can change the number before it's logged.

Notifications

Reminders are local notifications scheduled on your phone by the app. No push server is involved and no notification content leaves the device.

Subscriptions

LOCKD Pro is sold through Apple's In-App Purchase. Payment, renewal and cancellation are handled entirely by Apple. We do not see your name, email, or payment details; the app only asks the App Store whether a subscription is active on the device.

Photos

If you tap Download on a day card, the app asks for permission to add that one image to your photo library. It never reads your photos.

What we don't do

  • No network requests at all. The app never contacts a server.
  • No accounts, no sign-in, no email collection.
  • No analytics, no crash reporting SDKs, no advertising, no tracking of any kind.
  • No selling or sharing of data. There is no data on our side to sell.

Children

LOCKD is not directed at children under 13.

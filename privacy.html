PRIVACY POLICY · EFFECTIVE 2 OCTOBER 2026

LOCKD is a personal accountability app. The short version: your data lives on your phone. If you choose to sign in, a backup copy is kept with your LOCKD account so you can restore it on another phone. Without signing in, nothing leaves the device. This page spells out the details.

What the app stores

Everything you enter or the app calculates is stored on your device only, in the app's private storage:

  • Your lock (start date and length), habits, things you're cutting out, and the daily ticks.
  • Plan tasks, rep counts, training sessions and routines, journal and gratitude entries, and daily scores.
  • Food and weight you log, and the height, birth year, sex, activity level and goal you enter so the app can work out a calorie budget.
  • Settings, including which notifications you want.

The app also writes a plain-text copy of this data into its own Documents folder on your phone so you can copy it off with the Files app.

If you delete the app, everything on the phone is deleted with it. A cloud backup, if you signed in, stays with your account until you delete the account.

Optional account and backup

Signing in is optional. You can sign in with Apple or Google, or skip it and use every feature without an account.

  • What the account holds: an account ID and, if Apple or Google share them, your name and email address. With Apple's Hide My Email, we only receive a relay address.
  • What the backup holds: a copy of everything the app stores (the list above, including food, weight, the body details for your calorie budget, training and journal entries). It's uploaded while you're signed in, a minute or so after each change.
  • Why: only so you can restore your data on a new phone or after reinstalling. It is not analysed, shared, sold or used for advertising.
  • Where: with our database provider, Supabase, in the European Union (Frankfurt), encrypted in transit and at rest. Sign-in is handled by Supabase Auth with Apple or Google.
  • How long: until you delete it. Settings › Account › Delete account in the app deletes the account and its backup from the server permanently. Signing out stops further backups. Data on your phone is not affected by either.

Calorie estimates

When you describe food in words, the estimate is produced by Apple's on-device language model on your iPhone. The text does not leave the device. Estimates are approximate; you can change the number before it's logged. Your calorie budget is worked out on the phone from the details you enter; none of them are sent anywhere.

Notifications

Reminders are local notifications scheduled on your phone by the app. No push server is involved and no notification content leaves the device.

Subscriptions

LOCKD Pro is sold through Apple's In-App Purchase. Payment, renewal and cancellation are handled entirely by Apple. We do not see your name, email, or payment details; the app only asks the App Store whether a subscription is active on the device.

Photos

If you tap Download on a day card, the app asks for permission to add that one image to your photo library. It never reads your photos.

What we don't do

  • Without an account, no network requests with your data. The app only asks the App Store about your subscription.
  • No analytics, no crash reporting SDKs, no advertising, no tracking of any kind.
  • No selling or sharing of data. The backup exists only to give it back to you.

Your rights

Everything LOCKD holds about you is visible in the app, and the plain-text copy in Files is a full export. You can correct anything in the app, sign out to stop backups, and delete your account and its backup at any time in Settings › Account. If you live in the EU, UK or a place with similar laws, you also have the right to ask what we hold, to get a copy, to have it corrected or erased, to object, and to complain to your data protection authority. Write to the address below and we'll answer within 30 days.

SUPPORT

LOCKD is a lock-in app. You pick a number of days, what you'll do every day and what you're cutting out. Every day is scored against the one before, and the whole lock is drawn as a sphere that only grows if you show up.

Contact

Email lockd-support@googlegroups.com. Replies within two days. Your message stays private.

Common questions

Where's my data? On your phone. If you sign in, a backup copy is kept with your account (Supabase, EU) so a new phone can restore it. Meals and tasks you send for an AI estimate go through our server to Anthropic and aren't stored by us. Turn AI off in Settings › AI. Details in the privacy policy.

Get a copy of my data. Open the Files app › On My iPhone › LOCKD: lockd-export.json is everything the app holds.

Delete my account. In the app: Settings › Account › Delete account. If the paywall is showing, tap Account at the bottom of it. The account and its backup are deleted for good; what's on your phone stays. Deleting the app removes everything on the phone.

Cancel or restore my subscription. Subscriptions are billed and managed by Apple: iPhone Settings › your name › Subscriptions. To restore on a new phone: Settings inside the app › LOCKD PRO › Restore purchases. Refunds: reportaproblem.apple.com.

No notifications. Settings inside the app › REMINDERS › Send a test reminder. If nothing arrives, iOS has notifications off for LOCKD: iPhone Settings › Notifications › LOCKD.

I want to shorten the lock. You can't. That's the point. You can extend it, or reset everything from the profile screen, which saves a copy first.

Are the calories exact? No. They're estimates, and you can change any number. LOCKD isn't medical advice; talk to a doctor before big diet changes, especially if you're pregnant or have had an eating disorder.

LOCKD

SUPPORT

LOCKD is a lock-in app. You pick a number of days, what you'll do every day and what you're cutting out. Every day is scored against the one before, and the whole lock is drawn as a sphere that only grows if you show up.

Contact

Open a request at github.com/molecoder22/lockd-site/issues. Replies within two days.

Common questions

No notifications. Settings inside the app › REMINDERS › Send a test reminder. If nothing arrives, iOS has notifications off for LOCKD: Settings › Notifications › LOCKD.

I want to shorten the lock. You can't. That's the point. You can extend it, or reset everything from the profile screen, which writes a backup first.

Restore my subscription. Settings inside the app › LOCKD PRO › Restore purchases. Subscriptions are managed in Settings › Apple ID › Subscriptions.

Where's my data? On your phone. Nothing is uploaded. A plain JSON copy sits in the app's Documents folder, visible in the Files app under LOCKD.
